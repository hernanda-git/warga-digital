## Priority Analysis: Why These 8 Themes?

### Tier 1: Critical Missing Colors (4 themes)

These were the **highest priority** gaps in the spectrum:

#### 1. **Merah (Red)** - #d32f2f
- **Why Critical:** Red is fundamental for error states, alerts, and dangerous actions
- **Current Alternative:** Only Rose Pink (#c2185b) available - too soft
- **Use Cases:** Alerts, errors, delete confirmations, urgent actions
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

#### 2. **Kuning Emas (Amber/Yellow)** - #f57f17
- **Why Critical:** Bright yellow/amber is essential for warnings and attention
- **Current Alternative:** None - huge gap in warm spectrum
- **Use Cases:** Warnings, pending states, attention-grabbing features
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

#### 3. **Sian (Cyan)** - #0097a7
- **Why Critical:** Cyan fills modern tech-forward aesthetic gap
- **Current Alternative:** Teal exists but too muted
- **Use Cases:** Modern UI, tech-focused features, progressive actions
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

#### 4. **Indigo** - #303f9f
- **Why Critical:** Deep blue for professional/corporate applications
- **Current Alternative:** Medium Blue (#1976d2) - different personality
- **Use Cases:** Enterprise, formal apps, traditional designs
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

### Tier 2: Desirable Spectrum Completeness (4 themes)

These enhance the palette with variations and personality:

#### 5. **Jeruk Lemon (Lime)** - #9ccc65
- **Why Useful:** Bright, vibrant green distinct from default
- **Fills Gap:** Green spectrum variation (light/bright green)
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)

#### 6. **Coklat (Brown)** - #6d4c41
- **Why Useful:** Earthy, natural tone adds warmth and uniqueness
- **Fills Gap:** Earth tone spectrum (completely missing)
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)

#### 7. **Hijau Gelap (Deep Green)** - #1b5e20
- **Why Useful:** Sophisticated alternative to default green
- **Fills Gap:** Green spectrum variation (dark/forest green)
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐ (2/5)

#### 8. **Karang (Coral)** - #ff6f60
- **Why Useful:** Friendly, warm tone bridges red and orange
- **Fills Gap:** Red spectrum variation (warm/friendly red)
- **Priority Score:** ⭐⭐ (2/5)
